Research

Algebraic coding theory, combinatorics, rank-metric codes, and related questions in information security.

Research overview

My research focuses on algebraic coding theory and combinatorics, especially classical coding theory, rank-metric codes, and q-matroids. My publications and preprints examine topics ranging from almost affine vector rank-metric codes to the information-theoretic structures behind secure linear network coding and secret sharing.
