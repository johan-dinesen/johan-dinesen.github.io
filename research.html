Research

My research lies in algebraic coding theory and combinatorics, with a particular focus on classical coding theory, rank-metric codes, and q-matroids.

I am especially interested in the connections between coding theory and information security, including secret sharing, secure linear network coding, and the algebraic structures underlying error-correcting codes.
