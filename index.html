Johan Vester Dinesen

Doctoral Researcher · Aalto University

I am a doctoral researcher in mathematics in the Algebra, Number Theory, and Applications Research Group at Aalto University, Finland. My research is in algebraic coding theory and combinatorics, with particular interests in algebraic and combinatorial coding theory, secret sharing, rank-metric codes, and q-polymatroids. My doctoral advisors are Camilla Hollanti, Ragnar Freij-Hollanti and Eimear Byrne.

Before joining Aalto University in September 2023, I completed my BSc and MSc in Mathematics at Aalborg University, Denmark.
