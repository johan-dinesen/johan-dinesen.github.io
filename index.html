ACADEMIC PROFILE

Johan V. Dinesen

Doctoral Researcher · Aalto University

I am a doctoral researcher in mathematics at Aalto University, Finland. My research is in algebraic coding theory and combinatorics, with particular interests in classical coding theory, rank-metric codes, and q-polymatroids. My doctoral advisors are Eimear Byrne, Camilla Hollanti, and Ragnar Freij-Hollanti.

Before joining Aalto University in September 2023, I completed my BSc and MSc in Mathematics at Aalborg University, Denmark.
