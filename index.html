Johan V. Dinesen

Doctoral Researcher in Mathematics, Aalto University

I am a doctoral researcher in mathematics at Aalto University, Finland. My research is in algebraic coding theory and combinatorics, particularly classical coding theory, rank-metric codes, and q-matroids. My doctoral advisors are Eimear Byrne, Camilla Hollanti, and Ragnar Freij-Hollanti.

I received my BSc and MSc in Mathematics from Aalborg University, Denmark.
