Johan Vester Dinesen

Doctoral Researcher · Aalto University

I am a doctoral researcher in mathematics in the Algebra, Number Theory, and Applications Research Group at Aalto University, Finland. My research interests are in algebraic and combinatorial coding theory, in particular secret sharing, rank-metric codes, and q-polymatroids. My doctoral advisors are Camilla Hollanti, Ragnar Freij-Hollanti and Eimear Byrne.

Before joining Aalto University in September 2023, I completed my BSc and MSc in Mathematics at Aalborg University, Denmark.

Research

My research is in algebraic coding theory and combinatorics, with a particular focus on classical coding theory, rank-metric codes, and q-polymatroids.

I am particularly interested in the interplay between coding theory and information theory, as well as combinatorial interpretations of problems and structures arising in both fields.
